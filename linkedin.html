LinkedIn Email Grabber

A bookmark button. Scroll LinkedIn normally → tap it → all emails on the screen are copied. Then paste into the Email Scraper.

On PC (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Show the bookmarks bar: Ctrl+Shift+B
  2. Drag this button onto the bar: 📋 Grab Emails

On iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap Copy code below.
  2. Bookmark any page (Share → Add Bookmark), name it Grab Emails.
  3. Open Bookmarks → Edit → tap Grab Emails → replace the address with the copied code → Done.

How to use

  1. In LinkedIn search: "send your CV" mechanical engineer → filter Posts, sort Latest.
  2. Scroll down so many posts load. Tap …see more on posts if the email is hidden.
  3. Tap the Grab Emails bookmark → it shows how many were copied.
  4. Open the Email Scraper → paste into the box → copy CSV to your sheet.

Works on any page — Telegram Web, job sites, company pages too. Use it at normal browsing pace; it only reads what's already on your screen.
